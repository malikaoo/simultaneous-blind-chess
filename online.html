🌐 PeerJS & Commit-Reveal Security

This game connects directly device-to-device via WebRTC. To guarantee secret simultaneous moves without a central server, it uses a cryptographic Commit-Reveal Scheme:

  1. When you lock your move, your browser calculates an encrypted SHA-256 hash (Commitment) of your move + secret salt and sends it to your opponent.
  2. Neither player can read the opponent's move from the network traffic.
  3. Once both players submit their commitments, both games automatically exchange their actual move data and secret salt (Reveal).
  4. Each client mathematically verifies that the revealed move matches the commitment before executing turn calculations locally.

🟣 Purple Lock — No Repeat Moves

The piece you just moved is highlighted purple and locked for one full turn. You must move a different piece next turn.

💥 Collision — Same Square, Same Moment

If both players target the same square simultaneously, the higher-value piece wins and stays. If values are equal, both pieces are destroyed.

♙ Pawn = 1 ♘ Knight = 3 ♗ Bishop = 3 ♖ Rook = 5 ♕ Queen = 9 ♔ King = 100
